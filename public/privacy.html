Privacy Policy

iDisagree · Last updated July 24, 2026

iDisagree ("we", "our", "the app") is an argument-mapping tool that uses AI to structure and analyze productive disagreements. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it. It applies to both the web app at idisagree.trolleysolution.com and the Android app.

What we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email address Used to create and sign in to your account (via Supabase Auth). We don't collect your name, phone number, or any other profile information.
Password Stored as a salted hash by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) — we never see or store your password in plain text.
Your arguments / disagreement content The statements you enter and the argument map built from them (nodes, speakers, structure) are saved to your account so you can revisit them from the History tab.
AI credit balance & purchase records We track how many credits you have and deduct them as you use AI features. When you buy credits, we store the amount purchased and a Stripe transaction reference — never your card number.
Theme & sound preferences Saved only in your browser or device's local storage. This never leaves your device or reaches our servers.

If you use the app without signing in ("guest mode"), your work stays in the app's memory for that session only — nothing is sent to our servers until you sign in and it auto-saves.

How your content is used

Each statement you submit is sent, along with the current state of your argument map, to Anthropic's Claude API so it can generate the updated map, detect contradictions and rhetorical tactics, and power the Moderator tab. This is the core function of the app — your content isn't sent anywhere else, isn't sold, and isn't used for advertising. Under Anthropic's standard API terms, content sent to the API is not used to train their models. See Anthropic's own privacy policy (linked above) for details on how they handle API data.

Payments

AI credit purchases are handled entirely by Stripe. When you buy credits, you're taken to a Stripe-hosted checkout page — your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and the amount, so we can add the corresponding credits to your account.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app, each of which processes data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and server-side functions.
  • Anthropic — AI processing of your disagreement content (see above).
  • Stripe — payment processing for credit purchases.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and serves the web app.

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data security

All traffic to and from the app is encrypted (HTTPS). Your productive disagreements and account data are protected by row-level security in our database, meaning only you can read or write your own data — not even other signed-in users can see it.

Your controls

  • Delete individual productive disagreements any time from the History tab.
  • Delete all of your data — every saved productive disagreement and your credit balance — instantly and permanently from Settings → Account → Delete my data. Your sign-in (email + password) stays active so you can keep using it if you choose to; if you'd like your sign-in removed too, contact us below and we'll take care of it.

Children's privacy

iDisagree is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
